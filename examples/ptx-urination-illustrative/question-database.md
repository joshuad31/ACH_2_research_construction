# Question ID database

**ILLUSTRATIVE RETROSPECTIVE EXAMPLE — NOT A REAL ACH RUN.** Reconstructed from the existing corpus to demonstrate compact outputs. Questions and selection use hindsight. These records are for architecture research, not clinical conclusions.

Every IQ identifier is a question selected or rewritten for this example. Historical QIDs are ancestry, not claims that their original meanings were preserved. Within each entry, Raw → Transcoded is intended to preserve meaning; substantive retrospective revision happened before Raw. The Transcoded question is the one used to derive its witnesses. Questions apply to one linked validated FRD at a time.

## Question transcoding rules

This exercise uses a small controlled-English adaptation of PTX-URIN-CAN-V11-P2 and the C8 dictionary cautions, not a rerun of the full language-tool protocol. Expand locally defined names (R-001); distinguish endpoint names (R-015), within-group and between-group contrasts (R-017), eligibility and outcomes (R-025), and thresholds (R-026). Keep model, population, treatment, comparator, uncertainty, time and scope unchanged. Tamsulosin alone and PTX alone remain different meanings of monotherapy. A clear question can pass through unchanged. Per-entry notes identify the actual changes. No hypothetical rule equivalence or full dictionary conformance is claimed.
